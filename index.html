RetroType Keyboard is here

The typing you remember from classic keyboard phones, re-imagined for the Android phones of today.

Download RetroType (free) Become a Keyholder

Free. No account, no sign-up. Android 12 or newer.

Install it

  1. Go to the latest release and tap the .apk file to download it.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. Open RetroType and follow the short setup. It walks you through turning the keyboard on.

Rather get it by email? It's also on Gumroad: choose the Free version and pay $0.

About the install warnings: Android shows a warning for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. RetroType is registered with Google's developer verification, and since it has no internet access, it can't send anything anywhere.

Supported phones

RetroType is tuned for:

  • Clicks keyboard cases (Pixel 9 & Razr)
  • Dumber Mini
  • Minimal Phone
  • P26
  • Q25
  • Sidephone (Compact QWERTY tile)

It also works on any Android phone with its own on-screen keyboard. Keyholders vote on which phone gets support next.

What you can count on

  • No tracking. RetroType doesn't monitor what you type or collect any data.
  • No internet access. The app can't go online at all. Your words stay on your phone.
  • No ads. Not now, not ever.
  • No account. Download it and type.
  • Made by one person. Just me, with help from a small group of testers.

Getting updates

RetroType never goes online, so it can't check for updates itself. Two easy options:

  • Obtainium: add https://github.com/ReturningRetro/RetroType and it'll tell you when there's a new version.
  • Email: sign up here and I'll let you know.

Why I made it

Physical keyboards went from being the standard on phones to a clunky, retrofitted mix of hardware and software. The existing keyboard apps were okay, but nothing came close to the typing that was so common just a few years ago.

That reached a breaking point for me when a prototype QWERTY phone landed on my desk with no keyboard optimizations at all. So I decided to make my own. It turned out better than I ever imagined, so I added support for more phones and decided to share it with anyone who might enjoy it or benefit from it.

I've used these phones in their day, I use the modern ones now, and I've used just about everything in between. RetroType is built from that experience.
