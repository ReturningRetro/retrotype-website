Become a Keyholder

RetroType is free, and the free version is the full keyboard. Keyholders keep the project going, and get a few extras as a thank-you.

Become a Keyholder Back to RetroType

Whatever you want, starting at $10. Once, not a subscription.

What Keyholders get

  • More extras to unlock. On day one: the Cyberpunk theme, the Midnight app icon, a pink accent color and the Thock key sound, on top of everything in the free version.
  • New extras every month or two. Each feature drop brings a new theme and another extra, each behind its own secret, and Keyholders get every one of them.
  • The Keymaster challenge. Find every secret to complete it, with a celebration to match.
  • New versions a week early. Straight to your inbox from Gumroad.
  • Your name on the About page, if you want it there.
  • A vote on what's next, including which phone gets support next.

The money goes straight into test phones and new features.

Extras you find, not just switch on

In the spirit of the old games with their cheat codes, every extra is hidden behind its own secret. Type the right word anywhere, or find the right spot in the settings, and an extra unlocks with its own little animation. The Unlocks page gives you a hint for each one. If you'd rather skip the hunt, there's a skeleton key that opens everything at once (finding them all yourself completes the Keymaster challenge instead).

Privacy: RetroType never saves or sends what you type. Secret words are spotted the way spell check spots typos: on your phone, as you type. Only the unlock is kept.

How it works

  1. Choose the Keyholder version on Gumroad and name your price ($10 or more).
  2. Download the Keyholder app from your Gumroad receipt or library. Already have the free version? Install the Keyholder app over it; your settings, learned words and any secrets you've found come along.
  3. Tap Get your Keyholder code on your receipt, paste the license key from it, and choose the name RetroType should thank you by. Your code appears right away.
  4. Open RetroType and paste your code when it asks. Your Keyholder secrets are then ready to find; the hints are on Settings › Unlocks.

Your code is checked on your phone, with no internet needed. It's personal to you, so please don't share it. Lost it? Just claim it again with the same license key.

Questions

Do free users miss out on typing features? No. Every typing feature and every supported phone is in the free version, and always will be.

Is it a subscription? No. You pay once, and new extras keep coming with new versions.

Can I give it as a gift? Yes. Buy it on Gumroad as usual, then pass the Keyholder app and the license key from your receipt to your friend, so they can claim a code in their own name.

Something else? Email retrotype@returningretro.com. It's just me, so please be patient if I'm slow to reply.

Thank you for keeping RetroType going.
